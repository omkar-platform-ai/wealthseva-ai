# WealthSeva AI — Demo Script (August 13, Demo Day)

**Total Time: 5 minutes** | Practice until you can do it in 4:30.

---

## [0:00–0:30] The Hook

> "India has 500 million bank customers. Less than 2% ever receive personalized wealth advice.
> The rest? They rely on guesswork, WhatsApp forwards, and luck.
> WealthSeva AI changes that — right inside IDBI's mobile app. In any language."

---

## [0:30–1:30] The Problem

Show a slide: fragmented advisory landscape, advisor-to-customer ratio, vernacular gap.

> "Wealth management is a privilege for the urban English-speaking few.
> 70% of IDBI's customers are more comfortable in Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, or Bengali.
> No existing solution serves them in their language."

---

## [1:30–3:30] Live Demo

**Step 1** — Open app in English. New customer flow.
> "Meet Ramesh. He's 34, earns ₹80,000/month, and has never invested."

**Step 2** — Risk quiz (show 5 questions, animated)
> "Shreya — our AI avatar — guides him through a 60-second risk assessment."

**Step 3** — Risk profile displayed: Moderate. Allocation shown.
> "Shreya: 'Based on your profile, I recommend 40% equity, 30% debt, 30% FD.'"

**Step 4** — Ask: "What if I invest ₹5,000/month for 10 years?"
> Avatar responds with projection. Streaming text + voice simultaneously.

**Step 5** — **LANGUAGE SWITCH** (the wow moment)
> "But Ramesh is more comfortable in Hindi."
> *[Tap हिंदी in language switcher]*
> "Shreya switches instantly. Same conversation. Same context. Different language."
> Avatar: "रमेश जी, ₹5,000 प्रति माह के SIP से 10 साल में लगभग ₹11.6 लाख बनेंगे..."

**Step 6** — Transaction analysis
> "Shreya analyses his spending: 'आपने इस महीने खाने पर 32% खर्च किया।
> यहाँ बताया है कि ₹3,000 प्रति माह कैसे बचाएं।'"

---

## [3:30–4:30] Technical Innovation

- **Claude API** — natively handles all Indian languages. Zero translation cost.
- **RAG on IDBI's synthetic datasets** — grounded, factual, hallucination-resistant.
- **ElevenLabs multilingual voices** — same persona, 5 languages.
- **Deployed on IDBI's AWS** — production-ready, not a prototype.
